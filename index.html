SETUP COACH
    Setup tips for best results
    • Distance: stand about 1.5–2 m back so both shoulders and elbows are in frame, with room above your head for signs at face height.
    • Camera height: chest-to-eye level, tilted slightly up; shoulders should sit in the lower third of the picture.
    • Light: soft light in front of you (window or lamp behind the camera). Avoid strong light behind you.
    • Contrast: wear a plain top that differs from your skin tone and the wall; avoid busy backgrounds and long loose sleeves.
    • Hands: keep signs inside the frame, away from the very edges; slow down slightly at first.
    • Camera: use a 720p+ webcam at 30 fps. Close other apps using the camera.
    • Speed: enable hardware acceleration in your browser; on slow machines open the page with ?pose=lite.
